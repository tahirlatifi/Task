# Recruitment Proposal for ibex

**Prepared for:** ibex  
**Prepared by:** Seed Group  
**Website:** seedgroup-sa.com

## 1. Proposal Overview

Seed Group is pleased to submit this recruitment proposal to support ibex with qualified call center and operations resources. Based on the job descriptions shared by ibex, Seed Group will act as the recruitment partner responsible for sourcing, screening, shortlisting, and coordinating suitable candidates for ibex's final evaluation and hiring decision.

The purpose of this proposal is to define the recruitment scope, service approach, commercial terms, and approval section for both parties.

## 2. Recruitment Scope

Seed Group will recruit candidates for the following profiles:

- Customer Service Representatives - Junior and Senior
- CDD / KYC Operations Agents - Junior and Senior
- Quality, Training, and QC Profiles

Candidates will be assessed against the key requirements shared by ibex, including:

- Relevant customer service, call center, KYC operations, quality, or training experience
- Professional B2 English proficiency
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills
- Ability to work under pressure and accept shift arrangements
- Customer-oriented mindset, reliability, teamwork, and professionalism
- Typing proficiency where required for CSR roles

## 3. Seed Group Recruitment Support

Seed Group will provide end-to-end recruitment coordination, including:

1. Understanding ibex's hiring requirements, role expectations, and candidate profile.
2. Sourcing candidates through suitable recruitment channels.
3. Conducting initial screening for experience, communication skills, language proficiency, and role fit.
4. Shortlisting and sharing candidate profiles with ibex.
5. Coordinating interviews, candidate communication, and follow-up until final selection and joining.

## 4. Commercial Terms

Seed Group's recruitment service fee will be **8% of the selected candidate's annual gross salary**.

This fee will apply only to candidates successfully selected and hired by ibex through Seed Group's recruitment support. The final payable amount will be calculated based on the annual gross salary agreed between ibex and the selected candidate.
